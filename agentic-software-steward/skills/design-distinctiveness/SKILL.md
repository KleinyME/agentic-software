# Design Distinctiveness

Make specificity visible. Start from observed business truth, customer behavior, owner ambition, and the desired future, then invent freely. Artifacts are evidence of that larger reality, not the limit of what may be created.

Use this skill after `brand-direction` establishes audience, promise, and primary action and before `visual-direction` or frontend execution commits to imagery, typography, and page structure. `creative-director` remains the creative lead. This skill supplies observation and anti-sameness discipline; it does not replace taste with a checklist.

## Observe Before Inventing

Build a vernacular inventory with `references/vernacular-artifacts.md` before choosing the creative thesis, page physics, typographic voice, or signature element.

Design upward from business evidence. Do not design downward from an invented metaphor and then decorate it with facts. A metaphor is valuable when it reveals observed truth, customer behavior, owner ambition, or the desired future; it fails when it substitutes for research. Do not mechanically reproduce an invoice, sign, or old website merely because it exists. Artifacts are raw material, not preservation orders.

The core rationale must connect the chosen thesis, layout genre, typography, and signature expression to observed evidence or to a deliberate contrast that serves the audience. Minor reversible choices do not need citations.

## Run The Only-This-Business Test

Ask of the hero and each major section:

- Could this exact design serve a competitor after changing only the logo and copy?
- Do the central promise, hierarchy, interaction, and expression rely on business-specific evidence rather than decorative identity tokens?
- Would the owner recognize their shop, farm, kitchen, studio, crew, or product in the result - not only their name?
- Does the primary action preserve or improve the real way a customer books, buys, registers, calls, visits, or requests service?

If the first answer is yes or the others are no, return to the evidence. Change the thesis or structure, not only the palette.

## Let The Customer Job Set Page Physics

A scroll narrative is one genre, not the default. Choose structure from what the customer came to do:

- Menu-driven business: a document. Put menu, hours, location, and ordering within immediate reach.
- Visual product or transformation: a gallery. Let real work carry the page and keep interface chrome quiet.
- Class, event, or session business: a schedule. Lead with programs, timing, fit, and registration.
- Diagnostic or repair service: an intake or checklist. Start with the customer's symptom, job, or decision.
- Territory or seasonal service: a map and calendar. Make where, when, and response expectations concrete.
- Story-led or considered purchase: a paced narrative may earn its place.

These are starting genres, not a closed taxonomy. Business fit outranks forced variety.

## Decide The Site's Page Architecture

A single-scroll page with anchor links is one architecture, not the default. Before implementation, decide the page inventory explicitly and record why each page exists or why one page is sufficient.

Content earns its own URL when:

- A customer or another site would link to or search for it directly: menu, schedule, service area, individual service, wedding or event information.
- It grows or changes on its own cycle: galleries, seasonal availability, class sessions, past work.
- It serves a distinct audience or intent that should not share a scroll position with another: wholesale versus retail, parents versus dancers, new customer versus returning.
- It is the conversion surface: booking, registration, quote request, ordering.

Answer engines cite and retrieve URLs, not anchors. A one-page site gives assistants a single addressable unit for the whole business; a well-structured multi-page site gives a dedicated, citable answer per question. When machine recommendability is part of the offer, treat the page inventory as product scope, not styling.

A one-pager remains legal when the business is genuinely answerable in one sitting; it must be chosen against the alternative, not inherited from the demo form. Apply the same test as any other default: the choice must survive if the single-scroll form were unavailable.

When multiple pages ship: every page has a stated job and its own title and description, navigation stays consistent across pages, and no navigation link scrolls when its label promises a destination. Verify the intended routes with `release-steward` before presentation.

## Make Typography Carry The Voice

Name the typographic tradition and explain why it belongs to the business's world: industrial specification, humanist book serif, signage grotesque, rounded friendly, sign-painter display, typewriter documentary, or another defensible tradition.

Do not reuse the same pairing, scale system, or typographic mannerism by inertia. In batch work, follow `references/batch-rotation.md`.

## Use Authored Detail When It Is True

Use evidence-backed human trace or authored irregularity when the business's vernacular supports it: real handwriting, a staff mark, an uneven display rhythm, a photographed physical object, an owner annotation, a custom interaction, or another lived detail.

Do not manufacture crookedness. A business whose authentic language is precision may need no visible imperfection. Never repeat the same humanizing device across a batch as a house signature.

## Separate Heuristics From Hard Gates

Identity, contact and operating facts, asset rights, the working conversion path, technical invariants, and release state are hard gates at the stage where they become material. They may block public presentation or production, but they do not take over unrelated concept invention.

Artifact selection, layout genres, typographic traditions, metaphors, and authored details are creative heuristics. Break them whenever a stronger business-specific direction warrants it; record the reason instead of forcing compliance.

## Preserve Supported Claims

A fact stated by the business on a business-owned site or official profile is source-supported for concept work; it is not fabricated. Preserve useful certifications, services, perks, seasons, awards, owner names, and operating facts instead of stripping the business's earned proof.

Keep currentness, source support, and asset rights separate:

- Use source-supported facts directly in concept copy and keep the source in the working record.
- When authoritative business sources conflict, keep the disputed assertion out of a public owner-facing demo until the conflict is resolved; the conflict does not block unrelated creative work.
- Reconcile conflicting or time-sensitive phone numbers, hours, prices, staff, availability, and certification status before an owner demo or production release when the discrepancy could mislead or break conversion.
- Preserve a supported claim while resolving currentness. Do not replace it with a hedge sentence merely because confirmation remains.
- Reproduce certification marks, photos, and other owned assets only under the image and rights rules in `../visual-direction/references/image-use-and-truth.md`.
- When changing detail cannot be reproduced reliably, link to the authoritative business source.

Never invent reviews, pricing, history, certifications, awards, people, or outcomes. Never write in citation voice. The site asserts its own facts; it does not say "the official website describes."

Use the claim register in `brand-direction` and `brand-copy-steward` for approval and production status. This skill only prevents source-supported business facts from being mislabeled as fabrication.

## Govern Batch Work

For parallel concepts, `references/batch-rotation.md` is mandatory. Form directions independently, review repeated structure and signature devices as convergence alarms, and run the logos-hidden sameness review before presenting any site.

Batch diversity is a guardrail against agent convergence, not a roulette wheel. Never force an ill-fitting genre or type tradition merely to complete a matrix.

## Hand Off Demo Publishing

When a concept is published for prospect or client review, load `live-environment-steward` and follow `../live-environment-steward/references/demo-crawlability.md`. Public demos must remain shareable and machine-readable while instructing compliant search engines not to index them. Production promotion must remove custom demo de-indexing from the canonical production target.

## Working Record

Keep these with the concept's internal QA evidence, not inside the customer-facing page:

1. Vernacular inventory with sources, authority conflicts, and asset-use status.
2. Customer job, primary conversion path, chosen layout genre, and rationale.
3. Page inventory with each page's job, or the recorded reason a single page is sufficient.
4. Typographic tradition and pairing rationale.
5. Only-this-business evidence that remains meaningful after a logo swap.
6. Authored detail or human trace used when the evidence supported it.
7. Batch rotation record and reviewer sign-off when applicable.
