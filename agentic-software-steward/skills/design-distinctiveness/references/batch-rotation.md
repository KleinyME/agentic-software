# Batch Rotation

Parallel agents converge on shared defaults. Prevent accidental sameness without replacing business fit with forced novelty.

## Assign From Evidence

After each direction is formed independently from its evidence, record for each business:

- Customer job and primary conversion path.
- Layout genre suggested by that job.
- Typographic tradition suggested by the vernacular inventory.
- Candidate signature devices suggested by business-produced artifacts.
- Only-this-business evidence shaping the central promise, hierarchy, interaction, or expression.

Review directions as a cohort before implementation. Challenge repeated choices, but keep a repeat when independent evidence genuinely supports it. If two businesses need the same broad genre or tradition, differentiate their composition, proportions, hierarchy, interaction, and signature expression.

Business fit outranks completing a diversity matrix.

## Rotate Typographic Expression

Use named traditions to expose convergence: industrial specification, humanist book serif, signage grotesque, rounded friendly, sign-painter display, typewriter documentary, editorial serif, or another defensible lineage.

Treat an exact pairing, scale system, contrast pattern, or display mannerism repeated within a batch as high-salience convergence. Keep it only with an evidence-backed rationale and visibly different execution.

## Rotate Layout Genres

Do not default every site to a long editorial scroll. Let customer intent determine whether the experience behaves like a document, gallery, schedule, intake, map, calendar, catalog, tool, or narrative.

Repeated genres require genuine task similarity. Even then, compare information order and interaction shape for copied skeletons.

## Review Signature-Device Convergence

A signature device is a conspicuous recurring move: numbered rows, an italic hero word, badge cluster, marquee ticker, stamp motif, oversized year, decorative link arrows, handwritten note, tilted photo, or another memorable device.

Do not allocate one device per concept or let batch order determine creative availability. After directions are formed independently, treat a repeated high-salience device as a convergence alarm. Keep it when each business independently produces a compelling reason and the surrounding visual grammar remains distinct; otherwise revise the weaker use.

Never add an imperfection, annotation, or handmade effect solely to satisfy the skill. Human traces must come from evidence.

## Treat These As House-Style Alarms

These patterns are not forbidden forever. They block shipment when they recur by inertia:

- An italic-emphasis word in several hero headlines.
- Repeated two-part period-break headlines.
- The same hedge sentence across unrelated sections or businesses.
- Eyebrow kicker plus numbered rows as the default sectioning system.
- Repeated badge, coordinate, ticker, stamp, or arrow systems.
- Citation voice such as "the official website describes."

Fix shared structure, language, or typography. Palette changes do not resolve a house-style alarm.

## Run The Logos-Hidden Review

Before presenting any site:

1. Capture every hero at the same mobile width and one representative desktop width.
2. Hide logos and business names.
3. Shuffle the captures.
4. Ask whether each can be matched to its business from design evidence alone.
5. Compare headline formulas, information order, sectioning, type hierarchy, interaction shape, and signature devices.
6. Ask whether any two read as the same designer on a different day.

An unexplained repeat or a failed business match blocks the offending concepts. Revise thesis, structure, or typography before color.

Use a reviewer who did not create the concepts when possible.
