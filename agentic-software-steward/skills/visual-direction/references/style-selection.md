# Style Selection

Choose style from business reality and intended positioning, not a generic industry template.

## Decision Signals

Evaluate:

- Audience sophistication and context.
- Trust versus aspiration.
- Transaction speed versus considered purchase.
- Price and perceived risk.
- Tangible product versus invisible service.
- Need to prove people, place, process, product, outcome, or expertise.
- Desired emotional movement.
- Existing visual equity worth preserving.
- Competitive sameness to escape.

## Useful Registers

Use these as vocabulary, not presets:

- Documentary and human.
- Editorial and cultured.
- Tactile and material-led.
- Refined and restrained.
- Technical and evidentiary.
- Clinical and controlled.
- Warm and community-centered.
- Industrial and utilitarian.
- Playful and expressive.
- Cinematic and aspirational.

## Direction Test

A useful recommendation explains:

- Why this register fits this audience and promise.
- What proof the imagery should carry.
- What one visual idea makes the site memorable.
- Which category cliches it rejects.
- How it will remain coherent across hero, sections, social previews, and responsive crops.

Avoid adjective piles. Translate every trait into lighting, subject, composition, material, color, scale, and rhythm.
