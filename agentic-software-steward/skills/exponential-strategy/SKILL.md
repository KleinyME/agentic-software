# Exponential Strategy

Use OpenExO 3.0 as a conditional strategy and operating-model lens. Find compounding leverage, make purpose executable, and prove one real loop before expanding infrastructure or organizational scope.

This skill does not make every product an Exponential Organization. Product intent remains universal; an MTP, Intelligence Stack, or REWRITE program does not.

## Establish The Canonical Model

Read [references/exo-3-model.md](references/exo-3-model.md) before using named OpenExO concepts.

- Use the current official ExO 3.0 meanings of MTP, DRIVE, SHAPE, Intelligence Stack, and REWRITE.
- Treat ExO 2.0's SCALE and IDEAS as legacy concepts unless the user explicitly requests a 2.0 comparison.
- Never combine a stale acronym expansion with the 3.0 label.
- Treat book chat and other AI explanations as exploratory. Cross-check named concepts against the official model or living book before making them architecture inputs.
- Record the model version or source date when the recommendation could become durable project strategy.

## Put Intent Before Exponential Strategy

Start with the software steward's intent contract:

```text
For [actor], enable [outcome], proven by [observable evidence], without [unacceptable consequence].
```

Then distinguish three altitudes:

1. **Feature or workflow intent:** the concrete outcome being built now. Always required.
2. **Product strategy:** how repeated delivery, learning, data, judgment, or participation could compound. Required only when evidence supports it.
3. **Purpose protocol / MTP:** the durable organizational direction, non-negotiable constraints, and priority rules. Appropriate for enduring product or organizational scope, not a small feature.

If the user has not supplied enough business context to choose between a useful local feature and a strategic operating-model change, ask a short intention question. Do not infer that the user wants organizational transformation from their interest in AI.

## Run The Leverage Test

Read [references/opportunity-routing.md](references/opportunity-routing.md) when qualifying a proactive or explicit opportunity.

Look for at least one primary signal:

- Human coordination, routing, or status work is a larger bottleneck than execution.
- A repeated workflow has measurable outcomes and can improve from corrections or outcome feedback.
- Decision traces, proprietary context, curatorial judgment, or participant activity can become a compounding asset.
- The product can create a real network, ecosystem, or reuse effect rather than merely serve more users.
- The user is explicitly redesigning a workflow, function, or organization around AI from first principles.

Require a stable success signal or plausible verifier before surfacing the candidate. Helpful supporting evidence includes meaningful frequency, high waiting time, repeated exceptions, clean data access, bounded blast radius, and a reversible pilot.

Classify the result:

- `no_fit`: no evidence of compounding leverage. Stay with ordinary product and automation methods; do not surface an ExO suggestion.
- `leverage_candidate`: a plausible strategic mechanism exists, but economics, repetition, data, authority, or proof is unresolved.
- `intelligence_loop_candidate`: one recurring workflow can sense, decide, act, and learn with measurable improvement inside safe boundaries.
- `rewrite_candidate`: the current operating model is the constraint and the user has the authority, scope, evidence, and appetite to redesign it.

Do not surface `leverage_candidate` during unrelated work unless it would change the current architecture or is unusually high value. Never let a suggestion expand the active vertical slice without user agreement.

## Challenge The Baseline Without 10x Theater

When a qualified candidate may be trapped in a local optimum, use the software steward's `non_obvious_option` pass after a credible baseline exists. Require the alternative to name its leverage mechanism, evidence for and against, cheapest reversible test, success signal, prohibited actions, and stop rule.

Treat **Weirdo Pass** as a memorable operator label, not a new standing role or authority grant. The option may challenge assumptions and connect distant patterns; it may not act on tools, change scope, or outrank the accountable synthesizer. Reject novelty that cannot explain why the intended outcome should improve.

## Make Purpose Executable Only When It Earns That Role

For an accepted product- or organization-level purpose protocol, capture:

```yaml
purpose_protocol:
  human_direction: "<plain-language purpose that motivates and excludes>"
  hard_constraints:
    - "<actions, harms, or boundaries the system may never cross>"
  weighted_priorities:
    - priority: "<tradeoff dimension>"
      guidance: "<how to choose under pressure>"
  unresolved_conflicts: []
  accountable_owner: "<named role or person>"
```

Hard constraints must remain deterministic policy or permission boundaries where possible. Weighted priorities guide bounded judgment; they do not authorize a model to overrule law, user authority, security controls, or the prohibited outcomes in the intent contract.

## Use DRIVE And SHAPE As A Diagnostic, Not A Checklist

Diagnose the current bottleneck rather than scoring all ten attributes for ceremony.

- **DRIVE:** Decision Architecture, Recursive Learning, Intelligence Stack, Value Moat, Elastic Agency.
- **SHAPE:** Safe Autonomy, Human Architecture, Adaptive Architecture, Purpose Control, Ecosystem Trust.

Name the one or two attributes currently limiting the intended outcome. A fast intelligence engine without safe autonomy, human accountability, rollback, and trust is a blocked design, not a later phase.

For every automated decision type, specify:

- Who decides: deterministic code, model, agent, human, or hybrid.
- Whether the decision is reversible.
- Permission scope and source of truth.
- Confidence or policy threshold.
- Escalation path and named human authority.
- Evidence captured for evaluation and later learning.

Use `workflow-automation-architect` to choose the method at each step. Use `graph-engineering` only when the work has real parallel, conditional, durable, or relationship-heavy graph shape. An Intelligence Stack diagram is not evidence that a graph runtime is needed.

## Right-Size The Intelligence Loop

For an `intelligence_loop_candidate`, map the smallest real workflow:

```text
PURPOSE -> SENSE -> INTERPRET -> DECIDE -> ACT -> LEARN
                 GOVERN / ASSURE across every step
```

- `PURPOSE`: intent, constraints, priorities, and success evidence.
- `SENSE`: the minimum trustworthy signals required for this workflow.
- `INTERPRET`: context retrieval, deterministic transforms, or bounded model interpretation.
- `DECIDE`: explicit decision rights, thresholds, abstention, and escalation.
- `ACT`: the smallest safe action through code, integrations, agents, graphs, or humans.
- `LEARN`: outcome comparison, corrections, eval cases, and versioned improvements.
- `GOVERN / ASSURE`: trusted evals, searchable decision traces, granular rollback, and a named human review path for consequential exceptions.

This mapping may remain a document plus an existing application loop. Do not create an event bus, agent registry, control plane service, graph database, or multi-agent runtime until one accepted workflow proves a need that current primitives cannot meet.

## Use An Executive Cell As Decision Architecture

When the user wants CEO, COO, Marketing, chief-of-staff, or other executive agents, separate roles only when they own different decisions, inputs, outputs, memory, or evaluation criteria. Use the software steward's executive operating loop:

- CEO: refine ideas, compare opportunities, and choose or recommend direction within the standing charter.
- COO: convert authorized direction into bounded harness jobs, manage flow, and escalate exceptions.
- Marketing: contribute market sense, positioning hypotheses, and measurable experiments while respecting brand and publication gates.
- Hermes: activate accepted schedules or events; never act as strategic authority.
- Human owner: define purpose, charter, capital and identity boundaries, and consequential decisions.

This is a decision-architecture pattern, not proof that four persistent agents or a graph runtime are needed. Prove one recurring executive loop and its decision quality before expanding the cell.

## Choose Hard-Coded Or Intelligent Automation Deliberately

- Stable, exact, high-consequence rules -> deterministic code or versioned rule tables.
- Bounded unstructured interpretation with a strong verifier -> model call with schema, threshold, and fallback.
- Open-ended sequential tool use with a clear stop condition -> agent loop.
- Real conditional, parallel, or durable recovery needs -> task or durable graph.
- Ambiguous intent, weak verification, empathy, fiduciary judgment, or irreversible authority -> human.

Prefer a deterministic shell with small intelligent islands. Repeated model corrections should become eval cases and, when stable, deterministic rules. Repeated retries, corrections, and escalations may justify a read-only workflow-friction observer; treat its observations as evidence for the LEARN step, not permission for unreviewed self-modification.

## Adapt REWRITE Proportionally

Use REWRITE only for accepted workflow or operating-model replacement, not ordinary feature work. Preserve its sequence while shrinking its ceremony:

1. `backcast_define`: describe the future workflow and work backward from the intended outcome.
2. `assess_prepare`: establish current evidence, data readiness, authority, and the smallest safe on-ramp.
3. `extract`: capture the data, decisions, exceptions, and tacit judgment the workflow actually needs.
4. `diagnose_strip`: remove unnecessary routing, reports, approvals, and accidental work before adding AI.
5. `build_prove`: run one bounded workflow in shadow or parallel mode against predeclared success criteria.
6. `rewire_evolve`: migrate authority only after proof, deprecate the replaced path when safe, and feed outcomes into the learning loop.

For a solo founder or small team, default to one-workflow `micro` mode. Full-company Direct Mode or a separated Edge Mode initiative requires explicit organizational scope and authority. Never recommend a new team, CAIO role, or edge venture merely because the source framework describes one.

## Require A Proof Contract

Read [references/exponential-opportunity-contract.md](references/exponential-opportunity-contract.md) when a candidate is accepted for design or durable memory.

Before implementation, require:

- One workflow and one accountable owner.
- Current baseline and desired outcome.
- A stable success signal and prohibited outcomes.
- Data/source-of-truth and permission boundaries.
- Named decisions that remain human.
- Shadow, parallel-run, sandbox, or other reversible proof path.
- Evaluation, logs, rollback, time/cost limits, and a stop rule.
- The legacy or manual path that will be removed if the proof succeeds.

A canvas, readiness score, agent demo, dashboard, or strategy memo is not proof of transformation.

## Feed Project Memory And OB1 Carefully

Persist only qualified, durable strategy candidates. Use a stable key and update rather than duplicate them. Link a strategy candidate to its `automation_opportunity` record when one exists; do not create a second competing description of the same workflow.

Preserve observed evidence separately from hypotheses. Do not store private raw workflow content, secrets, invented economics, or generic inspiration in always-loaded memory.

## Required Output

For a surfaced candidate, provide an **Exponential Opportunity Brief**:

- Intent and current bottleneck.
- Classification and evidence.
- Compounding mechanism or reason there is none.
- One or two relevant DRIVE/SHAPE constraints.
- Hard-coded, model, agent, graph, and human method boundaries.
- Smallest intelligence loop or proportional REWRITE path.
- Human accountability, safety, and rollback boundaries.
- Baseline, proof metric, stop rule, and what will be deprecated on success.
- One next decision or experiment.

## Hard Rules

- Do not impose an MTP on a feature or a 10x claim on an ordinary product.
- Do not accept a non-obvious option merely because it is surprising or ambitious.
- Do not treat framework vocabulary, scores, workshops, or diagrams as shipped capability.
- Do not apply all ten DRIVE/SHAPE attributes as a checklist.
- Do not automate a workflow whose intent, authority, or verifier is unclear.
- Do not confuse organizational strategy, workflow method, and graph topology.
- Do not let recursive learning modify production behavior outside versioned eval, review, and rollback boundaries.
- Do not move humans out of consequential decisions without a named accountable owner and proven controls.
- Do not use OpenExO chat or third-party summaries as the canonical definition when official current sources disagree.
