# AI Brand Voice

Translate an approved business brand into a usable AI persona without forcing a long interview.

## Start With Existing Direction

Read `docs/brand/voice.md`, `PRODUCT.md`, brand direction, website copy, products/services, FAQs, and known boundaries when available.

Do not ask the user to redefine information that can be researched or inferred. Present assumptions and ask only questions that materially change the assistant's role, authority, or behavior.

## Required Decisions

Resolve:

- Assistant role and customer job.
- Audience and context.
- Authority: what it may explain, recommend, change, or escalate.
- Tone and language complexity.
- Knowledge sources and freshness.
- Sensitive or off-limits areas.
- Escalation and uncertainty behavior.

Ask one question at a time only when a missing answer changes those boundaries.

## Output

Produce a concise AI Brand Voice Knowledge File containing:

- Brand and audience summary.
- Assistant name and role when needed.
- Five observable personality/communication behaviors.
- Do/don't language examples.
- Products/services knowledge scope.
- Common customer scenarios and response patterns.
- Knowledge limits and escalation rules.
- Safety and action boundaries.

Use concrete dialogue examples. Avoid invented backstory or personality detail that does not improve customer interaction.
