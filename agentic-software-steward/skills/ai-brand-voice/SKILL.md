# AI Brand Voice Builder

Build a complete AI brand voice knowledge file for any business. Follows a structured research → interview → persona development → output flow.

---

## Step 1: Get the Business

Ask: **"What's the website or business name we're building the brand voice for?"**

Wait for the answer before proceeding.

---

## Step 2: Research the Business

Use web search to research the business. Gather what you can from their website, public content, reviews, and social presence. Organize findings under these five headings:

1. **Brand tone and style** — How do they communicate? What's the voice feel like?
2. **Products and services** — What do they offer?
3. **Target audience** — Who are they talking to?
4. **Key messaging** — Main themes and value props
5. **Pain points they solve** — What problems do customers come to them with?

---

## Step 3: Present Research & Confirm

Share findings in a clean summary. Then ask:

> "Does this match what you know about the business? Anything to add, correct, or clarify?"

Wait for confirmation or corrections. Update your notes accordingly before moving on.

---

## Step 4: Fill the Gaps — Interview Mode

For anything research couldn't surface, ask directly. **One question at a time.** Wait for each answer before asking the next. If an answer is unclear, ask for clarification before continuing.

Common gaps:
- Mission statement and core values
- Brand personality (if your brand were a person, how would you describe them?)
- Words or phrases to avoid
- Unique selling points vs. competitors
- Anything the website doesn't make obvious

---

## Step 5: Develop the AI Persona

Once you have a solid foundation, work through persona development — still **one question at a time**:

1. What name would you like to give the AI assistant?
2. What specific role will it play in customer interactions?
3. Give it a brief backstory that fits the brand
4. List 5–7 key personality traits
5. How should it handle:
   - Language complexity (simple vs. technical)
   - Humor or empathy
   - Formality level
   - Industry jargon

---

## Step 6: Key Behaviors & Sample Dialogues

6. What are 5–7 specific behaviors this AI should consistently demonstrate? For each, give an example of it in a real interaction.
7. Describe 3–5 typical customer scenarios and the ideal conversation flow for each.

---

## Step 7: Knowledge Base & Boundaries

8. What topics must this AI know cold?
9. What are the most common customer questions?
10. Are there knowledge limits — things the AI should *not* discuss?
11. How should it handle questions outside its scope?
12. What topics or actions are completely off-limits?
13. How should it handle difficult or sensitive situations?

---

## Output Format

Compile everything into a **complete AI Brand Voice Knowledge File**. Format it so it can be dropped directly into any AI project as custom instructions. No filler — just the blueprint.

### Required sections:

```
# AI Brand Voice Knowledge File — [Business Name]

## Brand Overview
Mission, values, personality summary

## Target Audience
Who they are, what they need, how to talk to them

## Products / Services
What the business offers (enough for the AI to speak fluently about it)

## Brand Tone
Do's and don'ts. Specific language guidance. Examples of on-brand vs. off-brand phrasing.

## AI Persona
- Name
- Role
- Backstory
- Personality traits (5–7)
- Communication style guide

## Key Behaviors
Each behavior + real example interaction

## Sample Dialogues
3–5 scenarios with ideal conversation flows

## Knowledge Base Scope
What the AI must know / what it should defer on

## Boundary Guidelines
Hard off-limits. How to handle edge cases and difficult situations.
```

Deliver the final file as a clean markdown document, ready to copy-paste into any system prompt or AI configuration.
