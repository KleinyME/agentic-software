# Platform Specifications — Historical Working Reference

Source: The 100 Year Copywriting Engine V13.001 — Ken "Spanky" Moskowitz / Ad Zombies

Always verify current limits and policy against the platform's official documentation before drafting, implementing, or launching. These are historical working notes from 2025, not a current compliance source.

---

## GOOGLE ADS

### Responsive Search Ads (RSA)
- **Headlines:** Up to 15 headlines, 30 characters each
- **Descriptions:** Up to 4 descriptions, 90 characters each
- **Display URL Path:** 2 fields, 15 characters each
- **Extensions:** Multiple types available with varying character limits

### Performance Max Ads
- **Headlines:** Up to 5 headlines, 30 characters each
- **Long Headlines:** Up to 5, 90 characters each
- **Descriptions:** Up to 5, 90 characters each

**Google Ads Writing Tips:**
- Pin your strongest headline to position 1 to ensure it always appears
- Every headline should work standalone — Google mixes and matches
- Use numbers and specificity in headlines ("Get 3 Free Quotes in 60 Seconds")
- Descriptions should expand on headlines, not repeat them

## META PLATFORMS (Facebook/Instagram)

### Feed Ads
- **Primary Text:** 125 characters visible initially, expandable to 63,206 total
- **Headline:** 40 characters (25 for mobile placements)
- **Description:** 30 characters
- **Link Description:** 30 characters

### Stories Ads
- **Text Overlay:** Minimal text recommended
- **Headlines:** 40 characters
- **Video Length:** Up to 120 seconds

### Reels Ads
- **Primary Text:** 125 characters visible initially
- **Headlines:** 40 characters
- **Video Length:** Historical working range was 15–90 seconds; verify current placement guidance

**Meta Writing Tips:**
- The first 125 characters of primary text must hook — that's what shows before "See more"
- Double line breaks in social copy preserve formatting when pasting into platforms

## LINKEDIN ADS (Professional Focus)

### Sponsored Content
- **Headline:** 150 characters
- **Body Text:** 600 characters
- **Call-to-Action:** Predefined options

### Message Ads
- **Subject Line:** 60 characters
- **Message Body:** 1,500 characters
- **Call-to-Action:** Custom button text, 20 characters

### Conversation Ads
- **Intro Message:** 500 characters
- **CTA Button:** 25 characters

**LinkedIn Writing Tips:**
- Professional tone essential — humor must be subtle
- Lead with value proposition, not company name
- Use emoji sparingly (0–1 max per post)
- Long-form native articles can be 40,000+ characters

## TWITTER / X

- **Tweet Text:** 280 characters
- **Promoted Tweets:** Same as organic
- **Twitter Cards:** Title (70 characters), Description (200 characters)

**Twitter/X Writing Tips:**
- Threads may suit complex ideas when the audience will follow the sequence; test against a concise single post.
- Images may change engagement; use them only when they add meaning and measure the result.
- Front-load the value — Twitter truncates early
- Avoid hashtag stuffing (1–2 max)

## EMERGING PLATFORMS

### Threads by Meta
- **Post Length:** 500 characters
- **Media:** Up to 10 images or 1 video (5 minutes)

### TikTok Ads
- **Video Length:** Historical working range was 9–60 seconds; verify current placement guidance
- **Text Overlay:** Minimal, focus on video content
- **Captions:** 2,200 characters
- **In-Feed Ad Text:** 100 characters

**TikTok Writing Tips:**
- Hook must land in the first 2–3 seconds
- Native-feeling content outperforms polished ads
- Trending sounds and formats increase visibility
- Text overlays should reinforce, not replace, audio

### Pinterest Ads
- **Title:** 100 characters
- **Description:** 500 characters
- **Video Pins:** Historical working range was 4–15 seconds; verify current guidance

## BLOG AND CMS

### WordPress/CMS Optimization
- Use proper heading hierarchy (H1, H2, H3)
- Include featured images with SEO-friendly filenames
- Add category and tag structure for organization
- Implement breadcrumb navigation
- Enable social sharing buttons
- Title tag: 50–60 characters (appears in search results)
- Meta description: 150–160 characters

### Content Distribution from Blog Posts
- **Email Newsletter:** Summarize key points with link to full post
- **Social Media:** Create multiple angles for different platforms
- **LinkedIn:** Focus on professional insights and industry perspective
- **Twitter/X:** Break down into thread format with key takeaways

## EMAIL MARKETING

### Subject Lines
- **Historical working range:** 41–50 characters for mobile preview; test with the actual audience and clients
- **Preview text:** 85–100 characters (shows alongside subject line)
- **Preheader:** Treat as a second subject line — it shows in inbox preview

### Email Body
- No hard character limits, but:
  - Above-the-fold message: First 250–300 pixels (varies by client)
  - Short commercial emails often use 50–125 words, but length should follow the message, audience, and measured results
  - Long-form acceptable when content is genuinely valuable

**Email Writing Tips:**
- Subject line + preview text must work together as a unit
- First sentence of body should reinforce the subject line promise
- One primary CTA per email — multiple CTAs dilute performance
- Mobile preview: 40–50% of email opens are on mobile

## VOICE SEARCH OPTIMIZATION

### Voice-Optimized Content Structure
- **Conversational Tone:** Natural speech patterns
- **Question-Based Headers:** How people actually ask questions
- **Concise Answers:** Direct responses to common queries
- **Local Context:** Geographic relevance when applicable
- **Featured Snippet Optimization:** Structure for position zero results

### Voice Search Power Phrases
- "How to..." / "What is..." / "Where can I..."
- "Best way to..." / "Why should I..." / "When do I..."
- Location-based: "Near me" / "In [city]" / "Local"
