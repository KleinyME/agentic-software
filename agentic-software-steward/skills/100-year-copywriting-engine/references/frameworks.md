Source: The 100 Year Copywriting Engine V13.001 — Ken "Spanky" Moskowitz / Ad Zombies

## TIER 1 — MOST EFFECTIVE FOR MODERN DIGITAL MARKETING

Use these first. They work across nearly every platform and audience type.

### AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action)
- **Best for:** All platforms, especially beginners and general persuasion
- **Effectiveness:** 95% success rate across channels
- **Use when:** General persuasion needed
- **Structure:**
  - **Attention** — Stop the scroll. Provocative headline or opening that interrupts.
  - **Interest** — Build curiosity. Relevant facts, story, or surprising insight.
  - **Desire** — Create want. Show the transformation and what life looks like after.
  - **Action** — Tell them exactly what to do next. Make the CTA obvious and low-friction.

---

### PAS (Problem, Agitation, Solution)
- **Best for:** Pain-point focused content, B2B
- **Effectiveness:** 90% for problem-aware audiences
- **Use when:** A clear problem exists and you want to drive urgency
- **Structure:**
  - **Problem** — Name the pain clearly. The reader must feel seen.
  - **Agitation** — Deepen the emotional weight. Show what happens if they don't solve it.
  - **Solution** — Present your offer as the logical, satisfying answer.

---

### PASTOR (Problem, Amplify, Story/Solution, Transformation, Offer, Response)
- **Best for:** Long-form sales copy, email sequences, high-consideration purchases
- **Effectiveness:** 85% for complex solutions needing relationship-building
- **Use when:** The reader needs to trust you before they'll buy
- **Structure:**
  - **Problem** — Identify and name the pain point
  - **Amplify** — Show what happens if they ignore the problem
  - **Story/Solution** — Tell a story that leads to the solution
  - **Transformation** — Show the before-and-after clearly
  - **Offer** — Present your solution with specifics
  - **Response** — Call to action

---

### BAB (Before, After, Bridge)
- **Best for:** Transformation-focused content, lifestyle and improvement products
- **Effectiveness:** 88% for products with a clear outcome
- **Use when:** A visible, emotionally satisfying transformation exists
- **Structure:**
  - **Before** — Paint the painful or frustrating current state
  - **After** — Show the vivid, desirable future state
  - **Bridge** — Your product/service is how they get from Before to After

---

### 4 Us (Urgent, Unique, Ultra-specific, Useful)
- **Best for:** Headlines, subject lines, social media—any attention-critical content
- **Effectiveness:** 92% for cutting through noise
- **Use when:** Grabbing attention is the primary challenge
- **Structure:** Every headline should ideally score on all 4:
  - **Urgent** — Creates time pressure or emotional immediacy
  - **Unique** — Says something that hasn't been said the same way
  - **Ultra-specific** — Uses numbers, names, dates—kills vagueness
  - **Useful** — Delivers a clear value or benefit to the reader

---

### H.E.L.P.F.U.L (Human-Centric SEO Blog Framework)
- **Best for:** Educational blog posts, long-form SEO content, authority building
- **Effectiveness:** 87% for organic search growth and engagement
- **Use when:** Creating people-first content that naturally satisfies search engines

## TIER 2 — SPECIALIZED HIGH-PERFORMANCE FRAMEWORKS

### QUEST (Qualify, Understand, Educate, Stimulate, Transition)
- **Best for:** Educational content marketing, authority building
- **Effectiveness:** 82% for establishing expertise
- **Use when:** Goal is to position as the trusted expert
- **Structure:**
  - **Qualify** — Identify and speak directly to the target reader
  - **Understand** — Show deep empathy with their situation
  - **Educate** — Provide genuine value and insight
  - **Stimulate** — Create desire for the solution
  - **Transition** — Move them naturally toward the offer

---

### APP (Align, Position, Prove)
- **Best for:** Positioning against competitors, differentiation needs
- **Effectiveness:** 79% for competitive market positioning
- **Use when:** Need to carve out territory against established players
- **Structure:**
  - **Align** — Agree with something the reader already believes
  - **Position** — Introduce your angle as the logical extension of that belief
  - **Prove** — Back it up with evidence, case studies, data, or demonstration

---

### OATH (Observe, Act, Transform, Habituate)
- **Best for:** Behavioral change content, habit-forming products
- **Effectiveness:** 76% for long-term behavior change
- **Use when:** Product requires the reader to adopt a new habit
- **Structure:**
  - **Observe** — Help them see their current behavior clearly
  - **Act** — Introduce the new behavior with low friction
  - **Transform** — Show the change in results
  - **Habituate** — Reinforce the loop; make the new behavior stick

---

### SCQA (Situation, Complication, Question, Answer)
- **Best for:** Business presentations, reports, B2B decision-maker content
- **Effectiveness:** 84% for logical, structured persuasion
- **Use when:** Audience expects reasoning before conclusion; rational progression required
- **Structure:**
  - **Situation** — Establish common ground; what's currently true
  - **Complication** — Introduce the problem, change, or tension
  - **Question** — Articulate what naturally follows ("So what do we do?")
  - **Answer** — Your solution, recommendation, or insight

---

### StoryBrand Framework (Don Miller)
- **Best for:** Brand messaging, website copy, brand clarity
- **Effectiveness:** 81% for reducing brand confusion and increasing connection
- **Use when:** Brand message is unclear or customer-focus is weak
- **Key principle:** Customer is the hero. Brand is the guide.
- **Structure:**
  - **Character** — The customer's goal/desire
  - **Problem** — External, internal, and philosophical problems
  - **Guide** — The brand as empathetic, authoritative guide
  - **Plan** — Simple path to success
  - **Call to Action** — Direct and transitional
  - **Success/Failure** — Show what's at stake; the transformation

## TIER 3 — CLASSIC AND SPECIALIST FRAMEWORKS

### Classic Frameworks (Proven, Less Modern)
- **4 Cs** — Clear, Concise, Compelling, Credible
- **4 Ps** — Promise, Picture, Proof, Push
- **FAB** — Features, Advantages, Benefits
- **ACCA** — Awareness, Comprehension, Conviction, Action
- **PPPP** — Picture, Promise, Prove, Push
- **SLAP** — Stop, Look, Act, Purchase

### Advanced Specialist Frameworks
- **Clayton Makepeace's 4-Legged Stool** — Four emotional levers that must all be in place
- **Gary Halbert's A-Pile/B-Pile** — Crafting mail that survives the initial sort
- **Eugene Schwartz's Breakthrough Advertising Principles** — Mass desire, market sophistication, and copy evolution stages
- **Frank Kern's 4 Day Cash Machine** — Email urgency sequence for short-window promotions
- **Jeff Walker's Product Launch Formula** — Pre-launch content sequence building desire before the offer
- **Perry Belcher's 21-Step Sales Letter Formula** — Comprehensive long-form sales letter structure

## FRAMEWORK SELECTION ALGORITHM

### By Platform
| Platform | Primary Frameworks |
|---------|-------------------|
| Email Marketing | PASTOR, PAS, AIDA |
| Social Media | 4 Us, BAB, PAS |
| Landing Pages | AIDA, PASTOR, BAB |
| B2B Content | SCQA, QUEST, PAS |
| Brand Messaging | StoryBrand, BAB, APP |
| Headlines | 4 Us, AIDA (Attention) + Power Words |
| Educational Content | QUEST, SCQA, AIDA |
| Blog Posts | H.E.L.P.F.U.L, QUEST, AIDA |
| SEO Content | H.E.L.P.F.U.L, SCQA, QUEST |
| Sales Letters | PASTOR, Gary Halbert direct response |

### By Audience Awareness Stage
| Awareness Stage | Best Frameworks |
|----------------|----------------|
| Problem Unaware | AIDA, StoryBrand |
| Problem Aware | PAS, PASTOR |
| Solution Aware | APP, BAB |
| Product Aware | 4 Us, PASTOR |
| Most Aware | Direct response, AIDA (Action focus) |
| SEO-focused | H.E.L.P.F.U.L, QUEST, SCQA |

### By Primary Intent
| Intent | Best Frameworks |
|--------|----------------|
| Persuade — Drive action or change beliefs | AIDA, PAS |
| Inform — Educate or explain | QUEST, SCQA |
| Engage — Build relationship or interest | StoryBrand, PASTOR |
| Convert — Direct sale or signup | PASTOR, BAB, 4 Us |
| Narrative — Tell a story | PASTOR, StoryBrand |
| Voice-Optimized — Voice search structure | QUEST, SCQA |
| Interactive — Engage through participation | QUEST + interactive elements |

## FRAMEWORK COMBINATIONS

- **H.E.L.P.F.U.L + AIDA:** For blog posts with strong conversion goals
- **H.E.L.P.F.U.L + PAS:** When addressing specific problems in educational content
- **H.E.L.P.F.U.L + StoryBrand:** For brand-focused blog content
- **H.E.L.P.F.U.L + QUEST:** For educational authority-building posts
- **PAS + 4 Us:** Headlines that drive problem-aware audiences
- **PASTOR + StoryBrand:** Long-form brand narrative with clear call to action
