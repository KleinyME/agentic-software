## A/B TESTING FRAMEWORK

### What to Test First (Priority Order)

**Tier 1 — Test These First (Highest Revenue Impact)**
1. Headlines and Subject Lines
2. Call-to-Action Buttons and Phrases
3. Value Propositions
4. Opening Hooks

**Tier 2 — Secondary Testing**
5. Email Preview Text
6. Social Proof Elements
7. Urgency/Scarcity Language
8. Power Word Combinations

**Tier 3 — Advanced Testing**
9. Story Elements
10. Personalization Variables
11. Interactive Components
12. Visual/Text Balance
13. Emoji placement and usage
14. List formatting variations

---

### Testing Implementation Protocol

- **Minimum Test Duration:** 7 days OR 1,000 interactions (whichever comes first)
- **Statistical Significance:** 95% confidence level required before declaring a winner
- **Sample Size:** Calculate before starting — underpowered tests produce false results
- **Variable Isolation:** Test ONE element at a time for clean results
- **Control Version:** Always establish a control before testing
- **Automated Feedback:** Set up collection before launching

---

### What NOT to Test Simultaneously
Never test headline AND CTA AND imagery at the same time. If a winner emerges, you won't know which element drove the improvement. Isolate variables.

## PERFORMANCE METRICS HIERARCHY

### Primary KPIs (Revenue Impact — Watch These First)
- **Conversion Rate** — What percentage take the desired action?
- **Revenue Per Visitor** — What does each visitor generate on average?
- **Customer Acquisition Cost** — What does it cost to get one customer?
- **Lifetime Value Impact** — How does this content affect long-term customer value?

### Secondary KPIs (Engagement — Leading Indicators)
- **Click-Through Rate** — Are people engaging with CTAs?
- **Time on Page** — Are people reading, or bouncing?
- **Scroll Depth** — How far are they getting?
- **Social Engagement Rate** — Shares, comments, reactions
- **Email Open Rate** — Is your subject line working?

### Diagnostic Metrics (Optimization — Use to Troubleshoot)
- **Bounce Rate** — Are they leaving immediately?
- **Subject Line Performance** — Preview text working?
- **CTA Click Rate** — Is the CTA compelling and visible?
- **Form Completion Rate** — Is your form killing conversions?

## BLOG-SPECIFIC PERFORMANCE MEASUREMENT

### Primary Blog KPIs
- Organic search traffic growth (month over month)
- Average time on page (target: 3+ minutes for long-form)
- Scroll depth and engagement (target: 70%+ scroll depth)
- Social shares and backlinks (authority indicators)
- Comment quality and quantity (community signal)
- Email subscriptions from blog posts (list-building signal)

### SEO Performance Indicators
- Keyword ranking improvements (track target keywords weekly)
- Featured snippet captures (position zero wins)
- Click-through rates from search (title + meta effectiveness)
- Page authority scores (domain authority growth)
- Internal link click rates (content discovery)
