# Writer Style Library — Complete Reference

Source: The 100 Year Copywriting Engine V13.001 — Ken "Spanky" Moskowitz / Ad Zombies

When writing in a specific style, internalize the voice characteristics before drafting. Don't imitate surface-level quirks. Understand the underlying approach to the reader.

---

## TIER 1 — MOST VERSATILE MODERN STYLES

### Gary Vaynerchuk (Gary Vee)
- **Characteristics:** Energetic, Motivational, Conversational, Real-time Marketing
- **Best for:** Social media, motivational content, B2C engagement
- **Audience:** Entrepreneurs, young professionals
- **Platforms:** All social platforms, email, video scripts
- **Voice notes:** High energy, stream-of-consciousness confidence, direct challenges to the reader, heavy use of "period" emphatic statements, unafraid of repetition

---

### Neil Patel
- **Characteristics:** Data-Driven, Informative, Actionable, SEO-Focused
- **Best for:** Educational content, B2B marketing, technical guides
- **Audience:** Marketers, business owners, technical professionals
- **Platforms:** Blogs, email courses, webinars
- **Voice notes:** Numbers and percentages anchor claims, step-by-step structure, heavy internal linking logic, every claim is backed by data or case study

---

### Ann Handley
- **Characteristics:** Conversational, Witty, Practical, Empathetic, Detail-oriented
- **Best for:** Email marketing, content marketing, brand voice
- **Audience:** Marketing professionals, content creators
- **Platforms:** Email, blogs, social media, newsletters
- **Voice notes:** Warmth without being saccharine, unexpected word choices that feel fresh, deep respect for the reader's intelligence, every piece teaches something useful

---

### David Ogilvy
- **Characteristics:** Research-Driven, Clear and Concise, Compelling Headlines, Long Copy, Benefits-Focused, Respectful, Storytelling
- **Best for:** Premium brands, sophisticated audiences, traditional advertising
- **Audience:** Educated consumers, high-value prospects
- **Platforms:** Print ads, websites, email, formal communications
- **Voice notes:** Never condescend. The consumer is not a moron; she is your wife. Research, research, research. Headlines do 80 cents of the dollar. Long copy works when you have something to say.

---

### Joe Sugarman
- **Characteristics:** Storytelling, Clarity, Emotional Appeal, Benefits-Focused, Direct Response
- **Best for:** Product sales, direct response, narrative-driven content
- **Audience:** Direct response prospects, story-responsive buyers
- **Platforms:** Sales letters, product pages, email sequences
- **Voice notes:** Every sentence's sole purpose is to get the next sentence read. Slippery slope writing. Establish an environment before the pitch. Make the product irresistible through story.

---

### Gary Halbert
- **Characteristics:** Direct Response, Conversational, Persuasive, Detailed, Storytelling
- **Best for:** Sales letters, high-conversion copy, relationship building
- **Audience:** Direct response buyers, relationship-focused prospects
- **Platforms:** Sales letters, email, personal communications
- **Voice notes:** Wrote like he was talking to one person. Highly personal. Used breeziness to lower guard then struck with desire. Famous for "the starving crowd" principle—the market matters more than the copy.

---

## TIER 2 — SPECIALIZED EFFECTIVE STYLES

### Seth Godin
- **Characteristics:** Inspirational, Concise, Thought-Provoking, Story-Driven
- **Best for:** Thought leadership, inspiration, change management
- **Audience:** Business leaders, change agents, creative professionals
- **Platforms:** Blogs, speeches, books, short-form content
- **Voice notes:** Ultra-short paragraphs. One idea, fully explored. Permission marketing perspective. Never shouts—whispers with authority.

---

### Joe Pulizzi
- **Characteristics:** Informative, Strategic, Analogical, Value-focused, Audience-centric
- **Best for:** Content strategy, B2B education, industry leadership
- **Audience:** Content marketers, business strategists
- **Platforms:** Industry publications, conferences, educational content
- **Voice notes:** Content marketing purist—build audience before product. Everything serves the reader. Strategic and structured.

---

### Brian Clark
- **Characteristics:** Informative, Conversational, SEO-Friendly, Persuasive, Content-Rich
- **Best for:** Educational marketing, organic traffic, authority building
- **Audience:** Entrepreneurs, content creators, online business owners
- **Platforms:** Blogs, courses, educational emails
- **Voice notes:** Copyblogger founder. Clarity above cleverness. Teaches while selling. The "teach and pitch" approach.

---

### Marie Forleo
- **Characteristics:** Uplifting, Empowering, Conversational, Practical
- **Best for:** Personal development, empowerment, lifestyle brands
- **Audience:** Women entrepreneurs, personal development seekers
- **Platforms:** Video, social media, courses, motivational content
- **Voice notes:** High warmth, direct encouragement, "everything is figureoutable" energy. Permission-giving tone.

---

### Alex Hormozi
- **Characteristics:** Practical, Value-Heavy, Data-Supported, Bold Hooks, Direct Response
- **Best for:** Business education, high-ticket offers, results-focused content
- **Audience:** Serious business owners, high-achievers
- **Platforms:** Social media, courses, business communications
- **Voice notes:** Lead with the most valuable thing. No fluff. Contrarian positioning. Dense value per sentence. "Give away the secrets, sell the implementation."

## TIER 3 — CLASSIC ADVERTISING LEGENDS

### Bill Bernbach
- **Characteristics:** Creative Revolution, Dialogue, Minimalism, Emotional Appeal, Surprise
- **Philosophy:** "If your advertising goes unnoticed, everything else is academic."
- **Voice notes:** VW Beetle "Think Small." The idea that honesty is the most powerful weapon in advertising. Respected the reader's intelligence through wit.

---

### Leo Burnett
- **Characteristics:** Human Touch, Simple and Clear, Strong Visuals, Emotional Appeal, USP
- **Philosophy:** Reach for the stars—you might not get one, but you won't come up with a handful of mud either.
- **Voice notes:** Marlboro Man. Tony the Tiger. Created iconic visual-verbal packages. Warmth and humanity in every piece.

---

### Rosser Reeves
- **Characteristics:** USP, Repetition, Simple and Direct, Hard-Sell Approach
- **Philosophy:** The Unique Selling Proposition — what one thing can you claim that no competitor can?
- **Voice notes:** M&M's "Melts in your mouth, not in your hands." Repetition until belief. No creativity for creativity's sake.

---

### Claude Hopkins
- **Characteristics:** Scientific Approach, Clear and Direct, Research-Based, Benefits-Focused
- **Philosophy:** Advertising is salesmanship in print. Test everything.
- **Voice notes:** Pepsodent. Schlitz beer (told the story of their brewing process which all brewers used—but he told it first). Pioneered the use of coupons and mail order testing.

---

## TIER 3 — DIRECT RESPONSE MASTERS

### Dan Kennedy
- **Characteristics:** Direct Response, Persuasive, Informative, Emotionally Engaging, Strategic
- **Voice notes:** Anti-institutional. Distrust of general advertising. Price resistance is a myth—value gaps are real. The power of the irresistible offer. Information marketing pioneer.

---

### Eugene Schwartz
- **Characteristics:** Direct Response, Long-Form, Persuasive, Emotional Appeal
- **Key work:** *Breakthrough Advertising* — the bible of direct response
- **Voice notes:** Copy doesn't create desire. It channels mass desire that already exists. Five stages of market sophistication. The headline must match where the market is in its awareness.

---

### John Caples
- **Characteristics:** Headlines, Benefit-Oriented, Testing, Clear and Direct, Research-Based
- **Famous headline:** "They Laughed When I Sat Down at the Piano—But When I Started to Play..."
- **Voice notes:** Benefit-first headlines. Specificity. Test against the control. Curiosity and self-interest are the two greatest headline forces.

---

### Clayton Makepeace
- **Characteristics:** Direct Response, Persuasive, Detail-Oriented, Emotional Appeal, Long-Form
- **Voice notes:** Health and financial copy specialist. 4-Legged Stool framework. Deep emotional engagement before any pitch. Every lead must achieve four things: stop, engage, create desire, prove credibility.

---

## TIER 3 — MODERN SPECIALISTS

### Joanna Wiebe
- **Characteristics:** Conversion-Focused, Clear and Direct, User-Centric, Persuasive, Data-Driven
- **Philosophy:** Copy hackers. Voice-of-customer research. Use their words, not yours.
- **Voice notes:** Founder of Copyhackers. "Your customers already know what to say—go find it." Surveys, reviews, and interviews as copy research.

---

### Drayton Bird
- **Characteristics:** Direct Marketing, Clarity, Persuasive, Emotional and Rational Appeal, Educational
- **Voice notes:** Worked with David Ogilvy. The offer is more important than the copy. Direct mail principles applied to digital. Relentless clarity.

---

### Ben Settle
- **Characteristics:** Email Marketing, Persuasive, Conversational, Informative, Frequent Communication
- **Voice notes:** "Email players" philosophy. Daily email. Infotainment—entertain AND inform. Never boring. Contrarian, opinionated, polarizing-on-purpose.

## KEN'S VOICE (AD ZOMBIES)

Ken's voice is its own entry. Read `references/kens-voice.md` when Ken's style is explicitly requested.

Quick summary: Confident yet playful. Sarcastic with purpose. Authentically approachable. Punchy phrasing. Fresh metaphors. No corporate jargon. No overused openers. No em dashes (unless explicitly requested).
